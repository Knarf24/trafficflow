How good is the forecast?

Trained on synthetic practice data (a made-up drive-through, 14 weeks). Models were trained on weeks 5 to 10, chosen on weeks 11 and 12, and scored once on weeks 13 and 14, which they never saw. Lower error is better.

Plan a new day (with live weather)

Forecast a day just after the practice data ends. Weather can come from Open-Meteo (free, no key) for tomorrow in any city, applied to the practice site.

Plan staff for a day

For each shift, every allowed mix of order takers and kitchen crew is simulated against the forecast and the cheapest is kept: wages + the cost of customers' waiting time + lost sales from cars that drive away + a penalty for cars queued on the street.

Count cars from a video or photo

A pretrained vehicle detector (NanoDet-Plus, COCO classes car, motorcycle, bus, truck) runs in Python with OpenCV on this device. Videos are stepped through 4 frames per second; each car is tracked and counted once when it crosses the red line. Nothing is uploaded.

Sample clip: “MD-355 and MD-124 Gaithersburg MD 2022-07-30”, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0, trimmed to 30 s and resized. The detector was used as is; it was not trained or checked on this footage, so watch the boxes. The sample shows a public road, so “Use as road traffic past the exit” fits it best.
